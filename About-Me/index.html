About Me

Hi, I’m Jaed Delosa. I may describe myself as ordinary, but my journey has been anything but. I began my career as a content writer for several U.S. companies and later worked with outsourcing firms in the Philippines, honing my ability to communicate ideas with clarity and resonance.

I graduated in 2020 from Caraga State University with a Bachelor of Arts in Sociology—a foundation that taught me to understand people, systems, and the bigger picture behind technology’s role in society.

Since then, I’ve carved my own path into the tech space. I am proficient in modern web development tools, AI automation, and Linux infrastructure management. These skills weren’t handed to me by a formal degree in computer science; they were earned through hands-on practice, iteration, and relentless problem-solving. I believe in learning by doing, and every project I take on reflects that philosophy.

Today, I’m focused on building a career where my technical expertise, adaptability, and drive for continuous growth can make a tangible impact. Whether it’s developing scalable systems, automating workflows, or bridging human insight with technology, I thrive at the intersection of creativity and engineering.
